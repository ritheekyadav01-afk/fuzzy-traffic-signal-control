PROBLEM STATEMENT

Fixed timers can't see the traffic

Every approach gets the same green time, whatever is actually waiting. One road queues up while the other sits empty.

1
Same green for everyone
A preset schedule cannot react to real conditions.
2
One packed, one empty
The signal cannot shift time toward the busy road.
3
Drivers pay in delay
Wasted green on one road is extra waiting on the other.
OUR GOAL: SET GREEN TIME FROM LIVE QUEUE AND WAITING-TIME DATA, NOT A FIXED NUMBER

HOW WE TRAINED IT

Fuzzy decides, GA tunes

1
Build candidate
A list of numbers (all membership breakpoints) becomes a fuzzy controller.
2
Test in simulator
Run it on the 40 training scenarios. Average delay is its score, lower is better.
3
Evolve
The GA keeps the best, crosses and mutates them. Budget: 2000 evaluations, same for PSO and SA.
4
Select and test
Pick the winner on 20 validation scenarios, score once on 60 unseen test scenarios.
